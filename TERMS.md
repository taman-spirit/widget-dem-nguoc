## Obligations of the embedding site

1. Do not remove, cover, dim or shrink the attribution line at the foot of
   the widget below legibility. This is what you give in return for free use.
2. Do not wrap the widget in another frame in a way that suggests the content
   is your own work.
3. Do not use automated tools to call the widget repeatedly and overload the
   service.
4. Do not extract content through the widget for reuse elsewhere, beyond
   displaying it directly to the readers of that page.

## Nhat Nguyet's rights

- We may disconnect the widget from a domain where we find a breach. That

- We may change the appearance or features. If we retire a widget that

  https://nhatnguyet.org/widget.
- We make no uptime commitment. The widget is provided as is, free, with no
  service level agreement.

## Data and privacy

The widget sets no cookies and sends neither the URL nor the content of your
page to our servers. The only thing recorded is the **domain** of the
embedding site, along with the widget id and the date.

One exception, stated plainly because it bears on your own obligations: the
**Your natal element** widget remembers an earlier result so a returning
reader need not enter it again. It stores only the **birth year**, in the
widget frame's localStorage, meaning on the reader's own machine. The frame
belongs to the nhatnguyet.org origin, so that storage is separate from your
website: you cannot read it and neither can we, because it never leaves the
reader's machine. Embedding that widget creates **no new personal-data
obligations** for you.
